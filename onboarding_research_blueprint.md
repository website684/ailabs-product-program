# BetterPlace AI Labs — Candidate Onboarding & Verification: Design Blueprint

## 1. Candidate Journey — Stage by Stage (chat-first, mobile, white-labeled e.g. "Subway Careers" on WhatsApp)

| Stage | UX Mechanic | Why (finding) |
|---|---|---|
| **0. Invite/Discovery** | Branded WhatsApp Business number ("Subway Careers"), JD broadcast to segmented pools, keyword-triggered auto-reply. No app, no form. | Text-to-apply lifts application-step conversion 20%→92%, funnel-wide ~3%→73% (Paradox); WhatsApp open rate 88-92% vs 18-22% email/portal (babblebots) |
| **1. Language + Consent gate** | First message: pick language once (persists through all modules) → immediately followed by itemized, plain-language DPDP/PDPA consent screen (not a checkbox), timestamped. | DPDP Rules 2025 mandate itemized independent consent; babblebots: consent must precede any data collection with audit trail |
| **2. Conversational screening** | 5-8 short questions, conditional routing, <10 min, answered inline in the same thread. | Screening completion 65-80% (WhatsApp) vs 30-45% (portal); text-to-apply screens complete in 2-4 min (gohire) |
| **3. Document capture** | Real-time sequential capture gate (presence→distance→blur→glare→tilt→occlusion→sharpness) with on-screen prompts; "confirm, don't retype" review screen highlighting OCR-extracted fields; graduated retry (score-banded, up to 3 auto-recaptures) before falling to manual upload. | BlinkID/Onfido cut blur/glare rejections up to 70%; HyperVerge's 0.70-0.89 auto-recapture ladder; 10-15% field-OCR failure tail must route, not fail silently |
| **4. Identity + liveness** | Country-conditional waterfall: India (DigiLocker→Aadhaar OTP→manual OCR fallback), Singapore (Singpass MyInfo autofill), Malaysia (MyDigital ID face-match vs JPN), Indonesia (NIK + selfie vs Dukcapil, no KTP photo needed). Passive liveness (<300ms, no gestures) by default; active liveness reserved for high-risk/inconclusive score only. | MyInfo verifies "within seconds," 35% adoption (Wise); Dukcapil skips KTP capture entirely; active liveness excludes motor-impaired users and hurts conversion (kby-ai) |
| **5. AI interview** | Text-chat (or voice-over-WhatsApp for India) structured 7-12 fixed questions, run in the *same thread/number* — no separate login or app. Show candidate a "why you were shortlisted" rationale trace. | HireVue's login gate causes 20-25% drop-off; Sapia's chat format hits 75.4% completion, 9.1/10 candidate sentiment; transparency ties to 4x retention (Fountain 2025) |
| **6. Background verification** | Candidate sees simple 3-state status (In Progress / Needs Your Attention / Cleared) per check (identity, address, employment, education, criminal) progressing independently; consent+ID captured as a hard gate before dispatch; candidate can proceed to training reels while slow checks (criminal, esp. non-metro) run in background; amber/red results get an in-app re-upload/dispute path. | Criminal checks alone take 2-7 days (SpringVerify); traffic-light amber benchmark 1-4% (IDfy); adverse-action dispute pattern (Checkr) reduces false rejections |
| **7. Microlearning reels** | Inline vertical-video feed (5-8 clips, 3-5 min each: role basics, safety, POS/till, customer interaction, Day-1 checklist), one MCQ inline immediately after each clip, in the same chat thread. Badge/score gamification. | WhatsApp-delivered microlearning: 85% completion vs 20-30% traditional LMS; 2-3x completion lift from short-clip+inline-quiz pattern (Axonify, edUme) |
| **8. Offer → Day 1** | E-sign/accept-decline as chat buttons; aggressively compressed offer-to-start window; automated 24h/2h reminders; visible progress checklist. Treated as its own tracked funnel stage with its own SLA. | Post-offer ghosting is the single costliest drop-off point, worse than any earlier stage; compressing 30→5 days cut attrition 62% (GoFor/Fountain) |
| **9. Post-hire (ongoing)** | Spaced refresher reels 2-3x/week feeding a rolling readiness signal into ops console, not a one-time gate. | Axonify: daily/spaced repetition drives 83% engagement, habit loop not binge |

White-label layer: brand name, logo, colors, and persona name skin the WhatsApp/chat surface per client org (Subway, etc.); underlying flow/engine is shared BetterPlace AI Labs infrastructure.

## 2. Ten Non-Negotiable Design Principles (ranked)

1. **Zero login/password/app-download anywhere in the candidate flow** — single highest-leverage conversion lever across every stage.
2. **Chat/WhatsApp-native, mobile-first by default** — 85% of frontline candidates apply from phone; design for 2G/3G reliability.
3. **Consent-before-collection, itemized, timestamped, per data category** — hard gate, never a footer checkbox.
4. **State persists to phone number/WhatsApp ID, not a session cookie** — resumable mid-flow across days/devices with zero re-entry.
5. **Language selected once at flow start, persists across every module** (screening, docs, reels, interview).
6. **Confirm-don't-retype** — every OCR/DPI-fetched field shown pre-filled for a visual scan-and-correct, never re-keyed.
7. **Passive-by-default biometrics; active/step-up liveness only for the flagged high-risk tier.**
8. **Candidate-facing status is always a simple 3-state view; internal adjudication complexity (risk scores, discrepancy notes) never leaks to candidate UI.**
9. **Every check/module runs its own independent progress bar and SLA clock** — never block the whole journey on the slowest component (esp. criminal checks).
10. **Offer-to-Day-1 is a first-class tracked stage with its own nudge cadence** — not an afterthought after "offer sent."

## 3. Admin/Ops Console — Must-Haves

- **Queue-of-queues**: saved filtered views by status/tag/risk-signal/country/BU (Persona pattern) — not one flat candidate list.
- **Two-tier status per candidate**: simple external state vs internal adjudication state machine (eligible/review/escalated) with discrepancy notes.
- **Independent per-check progress + SLA clock** (identity, address, employment, education, criminal) with breach thresholds — no single global timer.
- **Traffic-light triage view** (green/amber/red), amber rate tracked against 1-4% benchmark, auto-routes to human review queue.
- **Deep case-review UI** for the ~5% needing a human: side-by-side doc image, extracted fields, match/no-match flags, one-click approve/reject/escalate with reason code.
- **Auto-ticket creation** (Jira/ServiceNow-style) fired at the moment of a fraud/high-risk signal, not discovered later in a queue.
- **Funnel + stage-conversion dashboard**, with offer→Day-1 ghosting as its own tracked KPI.
- **DPI adoption/benchmark stats per country** (MyInfo/DigiLocker/Dukcapil usage %, time and cost saved) — what finance/ops will ask for to justify DPI investment.
- **Compliance-metadata audit layer**: consent timestamp + purpose-list version, retention/deletion due-date, masked-ID-only flag, per candidate — queryable for regulator inquiries.
- **No-code workflow builder** so ops can adjust routing/thresholds/strictness (per doc type, per market) without engineering.
- **Reels/training completion + quiz score** as a Day-1-readiness signal feeding the go/no-go, not just a completion checkbox.

## 4. Compliance Requirements That MUST Appear in the Candidate Flow

- **Itemized consent screen** before any data collection — plain language, purpose-listed, independent of other T&Cs, with a visible manage/withdraw link (DPDP Rules 2025).
- **Separate explicit opt-in for biometric/facial capture**, distinct from general consent (Malaysia PDPA Amendment 2024, Indonesia UU PDP).
- **Timestamped, versioned audit trail** of every consent event.
- **Aadhaar masking**: collect/display last-4-only; full 12-digit number never stored; ops console shows match/no-match, not the number.
- **NRIC (Singapore) never requested at application stage** — field gated to unlock only after offer acceptance.
- **Retention/deletion microcopy under every biometric capture screen**: why collected, how long retained, auto-purge date.
- **Auto-purge job** for national IDs tied to employment status change (no fixed statutory period — burden is on us to justify and enforce one).
- **Country-localized consent copy** (not translated legal boilerplate) — plain-language requirement is explicit in DPDP Rules.
- **BGV consent/LOA as a discrete timestamped gate** before dispatching any check (India Letter of Authorization; Malaysia signed consent + MyKad/passport copy).
- **Sensitive-data classification flag** per field (biometric = sensitive) driving stricter storage/encryption handling.

## 5. Top 5 Differentiators to Steal

1. **Text-to-apply, zero-form entry** (Paradox/gohire) — 92% vs 20% step conversion; the single highest-leverage mechanic in the whole journey.
2. **DPI-first identity waterfall** — Singpass MyInfo ("seconds," 35% adoption), DigiLocker's PKI-trusted fetch, Dukcapil NIK+selfie — genuinely skip document capture wherever government infra allows it.
3. **Chat-native microlearning reels with inline quiz** (WhatsApp pattern) — 85% completion vs 20-30% LMS, no separate app.
4. **Sapia-style explainable scoring trace** shown to the candidate ("why you were shortlisted") — doubles as bias-audit evidence and drives the 4x retention lift tied to perceived transparency.
5. **Checkr/HireRight two-tier status architecture** — simple candidate state decoupled from rich internal adjudication — lets ops run sophisticated triage without ever leaking risk labels into the candidate chat.

# Appendix: Research findings by dimension


## chat-first-onboarding

- **[number]** Text-to-apply flows (no forms, no logins, no passwords) lift stage-conversion from ~20% to 92% at the application step alone, and overall funnel conversion from ~3% to ~73% across application→schedule→interview→onboarding; candidates needed per hire drops from 30+ to under 2. The gain compounds stage-over-stage rather than being additive.  
  Source: https://www.paradox.ai/blog/a-frontline-applicant-tracking-system-that-cuts-candidate-dropoff-by-72-its-no-pipe-dream-its-real-its-ai-and-its-spectacular
- **[number]** Text-to-apply completion rates run 75-85% versus 2-6% for traditional multi-step web forms (one healthcare case went 4%→78%); the mechanic is texting a keyword, an AI instantly replying, then a 5-10 question conversational screen completed in 2-4 minutes total.  
  Source: https://gohire.com/ai-text-recruiting/
- **[number]** Paradox Olivia routes candidates to chat, SMS, or WhatsApp based on channel of origin and sends automated reminders specifically to cut interview no-shows; time-to-interview for hourly roles fell from an average 6 days to under 12 hours after deployment.  
  Source: https://thedailyhire.com/tools/paradox-olivia-conversational-ai-recruiting-review
- **[number]** Fountain's data: 85% of frontline candidates apply from a phone and 60% abandon applications that feel too long or aren't mobile-optimized; UPS reached 98% I-9 completion by Day 1 after moving onboarding into a mobile-first chat flow, and Stitch Fix raised Day-1 show rate from 68% to 95% with the same redesign.  
  Source: https://www.fountain.com/posts/candidate-experience-metrics
- **[pattern]** The single costliest drop-off point in frontline hiring is post-offer 'ghosting' between offer and Day 1 — worse than any earlier funnel stage — and employers close it by compressing the offer-to-start onboarding window (e.g. GoFor cut it from 30 days to 5, reducing attrition by 62%).  
  Source: https://www.fountain.com/posts/candidate-experience-metrics
- **[mechanic]** India WhatsApp hiring flows follow a fixed 5-stage pipeline (JD broadcast to segmented pools → chat screening with conditional routing → interview scheduling with slot-pick + calendar sync → stage-based status nudges → offer delivery via e-sign/accept-decline buttons), with screening capped at 5-8 questions and under 10 minutes, and reminders fired at 24h and 2h before interviews.  
  Source: https://babblebots.ai/blog/whatsapp-hiring-enterprise-india-2026
- **[number]** Reported channel comparison for India frontline hiring: WhatsApp open rates 88-92% vs 18-22% email/portal; screening completion 65-80% vs 30-45%; interview no-show 10-15% vs 35-40%; 48-hour offer acceptance 72-85% vs 40-55%. Save/resume works via a searchable, timestamped per-candidate conversation log rather than a stateful web session, and language is selected once at flow start (Hindi/Tamil/Telugu/Bengali/Marathi/Kannada) then persists.  
  Source: https://babblebots.ai/blog/whatsapp-hiring-enterprise-india-2026
- **[requirement]** Consent for personal data must be captured explicitly before any data collection starts in the chat flow, with a timestamped audit trail, to meet India's DPDP Act 2023 — implemented as an early, mandatory conversational step rather than a footer checkbox.  
  Source: https://babblebots.ai/blog/whatsapp-hiring-enterprise-india-2026

**Design implications:** Build the first screen as a text-message-style Q&A (5-8 short questions, <10 min) with zero account/password creation — this is the single highest-leverage lever on completion, not later polish. · Treat 'offer accepted → Day 1 start' as its own tracked funnel stage with its own nudge cadence (24h/2h reminders, progress-visible checklist) since ghosting there costs more than any earlier drop-off; compress this window aggressively. · Make save/resume implicit: persist state as a running conversation log keyed to phone number/WhatsApp ID, not a session cookie, so a candidate can close the app and pick up mid-flow days later without re-entering anything. · Ask for language preference once, in the very first message, and carry it through document upload, screening, and reels — don't re-ask per module. · Put consent/DPDP capture as an explicit early conversational step (not a buried T&C checkbox), timestamped and logged, before any document or ID collection begins. · Design against a WhatsApp-native channel as the default surface (not just an in-app chat UI) given the 88-92% open-rate vs 18-22% email/portal gap reported for India — even if the core app is native, mirror status nudges and reminders into WhatsApp.

## doc-capture-ocr

- **[number]** Onfido's Smart Capture SDK does real-time, on-device glare and blur detection plus auto-capture (passport auto-capture specifically), and its image-enhancement mode captures a short video then auto-selects the sharpest frame, cutting rejections due to blur/glare/positioning by up to 70%.  
  Source: https://onfido.com/solutions/smart-capture-sdk/
- **[mechanic]** Microblink BlinkID's capture pipeline runs a sequential chain of on-device checks before accepting a frame: document-presence + distance detection, single/double-sided type classification, blur detection, glare detection, tilt estimation, hand-occlusion detection, and multi-frame sharpness filtering (grabs several frames from the live video stream and keeps the sharpest) — each with real-time on-screen prompts like 'move back' or 'hold still'.  
  Source: https://microblink.com/resources/blog/building-blinkid-capture-even-better-ux-and-data-extraction/
- **[requirement]** BlinkID 6.8.0 added adjustable strictness levels (relaxed / normal / strict) for glare detection so integrators can tune the false-rejection vs. fraud-catch tradeoff per document type rather than using one global threshold.  
  Source: https://microblink.com/resources/blog/blinkid-6-8-0-release-glare-and-blur-detection-for-capturing-the-highest-quality-image/
- **[number]** HyperVerge's OCR engine is template-agnostic (works without pre-mapped document layouts) and claims 90%+ accuracy for arbitrary global documents, 95%+ for global ID cards, and 99.5% for India-specific documents like PAN card, where high accuracy directly raises straight-through-processing (STP) rates.  
  Source: https://hyperverge.co/in/solutions/ocr-software/
- **[mechanic]** HyperVerge's liveness/capture retry logic is scored, not binary: a confidence score of 0.70-0.89 triggers an automatic re-capture prompt with up to 3 attempts before the flow falls back to active liveness or manual review — i.e. a graduated retry ladder rather than instant hard-fail.  
  Source: https://javajack.github.io/broking-kyc/vendors/verification/hyperverge/
- **[number]** Onfido's Atlas decisioning engine completes 95% of document checks in under 10 seconds, and its 'Flash' route completes in under 15 seconds end-to-end; the vendor claims this speed lifts onboarding conversion by up to 40% by minimizing the wait window where users abandon.  
  Source: https://www.fintechfutures.com/press-releases/onfido-s-real-identity-platform-improves-performance-by-12x-with-fully-automated-end-to-end-identity-verification
- **[number]** Field-level OCR success on ID documents in real-world India KYC flows runs 85-90% for a clean/clear photo, leaving a 10-15% failure tail (blur, glare, damaged/worn cards, low-light) that must route to a manual correction or re-capture step rather than silently failing.  
  Source: https://www.befisc.com/fintechsherlock/digilocker-kyc-verification-india/
- **[pattern]** For document verification vendor selection resilience in India/SEA, production KYC stacks commonly chain 2-3 providers/methods with fallback logic (e.g. CKYC lookup -> Aadhaar eKYC -> video KYC -> manual document upload) rather than relying on a single OCR/IDV vendor, because completion and fraud outcomes depend more on routing logic across methods than on any one vendor's accuracy.  
  Source: https://surepass.io/blog/top-10-kyc-companies-for-digital-lenders-in-india/

**Design implications:** Build capture as a sequential real-time gate (presence -> distance -> blur -> glare -> tilt -> occlusion -> sharpness), not a single 'take photo then validate server-side' step — this is what lets BlinkID/Onfido reject bad frames before upload and avoid a slow retry round-trip over 2G/3G. · Use graduated retry logic (score-banded, e.g. HyperVerge's 0.70-0.89 auto-recapture-with-3-attempts pattern) rather than a binary pass/fail, so borderline captures (common with low-end Android cameras used by frontline candidates) get a fast in-app nudge instead of falling through to slow manual review. · Always insert a 'confirm, don't retype' review screen after OCR extraction — show the extracted fields pre-filled with a visual highlight/pulse so the candidate scans and corrects rather than re-keying, and explicitly budget for a 10-15% manual-correction fallback rate on ID fields given real-world blur/glare/damage rates. · Make strictness (glare/blur thresholds) configurable per document type and per market (Malaysia MyKad vs India Aadhaar/PAN vs Indonesia KTP) rather than a single global threshold, since document materials and lighting conditions vary a lot across markets. · Design for a fallback chain, not a single OCR vendor: primary auto-capture+OCR, then assisted re-capture, then manual document upload with human review — so a single vendor's accuracy ceiling doesn't become a hard onboarding blocker. · Set a speed bar for candidates: best-in-class flows resolve document checks in under 10-15 seconds; anything materially slower on typical frontline-worker devices/networks should be treated as a drop-off risk to actively engineer against.

## identity-liveness-dpi

- **[pattern]** Liveness detection is splitting into passive-by-default with active escalation only for high-risk cases: passive analysis (skin texture, light behavior, involuntary micro-movements) runs on a normal selfie in under 300ms with zero user action, while active liveness (turn head, smile, blink on command) is reserved for step-up checks — because active challenges exclude users with motor impairments and add friction that hurts mobile conversion.  
  Source: https://kby-ai.com/liveness-detection-in-kyc-why-passive-vs-active-methods/
- **[mechanic]** India's compliant KYC waterfall pattern is: DigiLocker pull first (issuer-signed, PKI-verified document — no OCR or tamper-analysis needed since UIDAI/issuer digital signature is trusted directly), fall back to Aadhaar OTP-based eKYC second, fall back to manual OCR document upload with forgery detection only as the last resort — each step triggered by a specific failure (no linked DigiLocker documents, OTP/service timeout, or stale/revoked doc).  
  Source: https://hyperverge.co/blog/power-of-video-kyc-through-c-kyc-and-digilocker/
- **[mechanic]** DigiLocker fetch does NOT eliminate selfie/liveness — it only replaces the document-upload+OCR+forgery-check step. Face-match/liveness remains a separate mandatory step layered on top (e.g. in Video-KYC/V-CIP, an agent or automated system still checks the live face against the fetched document photo).  
  Source: https://hyperverge.co/blog/power-of-video-kyc-through-c-kyc-and-digilocker/
- **[number]** Video-KYC calls (agent-assisted identity + liveness) run to an average call duration of ~70 seconds with 88-95% conversion rate reported by one vendor — a concrete benchmark for how short a live-assisted verification step can be while still converting well.  
  Source: https://hyperverge.co/blog/power-of-video-kyc-through-c-kyc-and-digilocker/
- **[number]** Singapore's Singpass MyInfo is the clearest true document-upload-skip case: after Singpass biometric/OTP login and one consent screen, MyInfo returns government-verified name, NRIC, address, date of birth, and (per Regtank/MAS sources) employment/CPF data directly to the onboarding form as autofill — DBS reports customer verification completing 'within seconds' and Wise saw ~35% of customers choose this path within two months with a 30% cut in verification cost, with no physical document or separate photo required for the KYC checks it covers.  
  Source: https://regtank.medium.com/how-myinfo-can-be-used-to-streamline-kyc-personal-data-collection-9b018e5c1c03
- **[mechanic]** Malaysia's MyDigital ID does not replace the physical MyKad — it verifies identity by checking a live facial scan plus MyKad image capture against the National Registration Department (JPN) database, either remotely (eKYC: MyKad image + face scan) or in person at kiosks (card + fingerprint). As of mid-2026 the government is upgrading kiosks with real-time facial biometric verification against JPN data specifically to combat identity fraud, and MyDigital ID is being rolled into MyJPJ and telco apps as the standard 'Sign in with MyDigital ID' layer — signaling DPI-linked face-match is becoming the default reuse pattern across MY government/private services, not just a one-off onboarding step.  
  Source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/malaysia-to-upgrade-mydigital-id-kiosks-with-facial-biometric-verification
- **[mechanic]** Indonesia's non-document verification flow is a 3-step mechanic with no ID photo needed at all: user types their 16-digit NIK number, takes a real-time selfie, and the selfie is matched against the photo already on file in the Dukcapil population registry, producing a similarity/confidence score — genuinely skipping KTP photo capture entirely, not just OCR.  
  Source: https://didit.me/blog/non-doc-verification-indonesia-ar/
- **[number]** One Indonesian eKYC vendor (IST) advertises 99.7% accuracy combining OCR + face liveness + Dukcapil-matched face recognition as a single bundled claim, illustrating that in the Indonesian market OCR-based document capture and Dukcapil face-match are typically sold/positioned as one fused step rather than sequential fallbacks (unlike India's DigiLocker-then-eKYC waterfall).  
  Source: https://ist.id/e-kyc/

**Design implications:** Build the identity step as a country-conditional waterfall, not one universal flow: SG (Singpass MyInfo) and ID (NIK+selfie vs Dukcapil) can genuinely skip document upload entirely for most fields; India should default to DigiLocker-first with Aadhaar-OTP and manual-OCR as explicit fallbacks, each triggered by a detectable failure state (no linked docs, timeout, stale doc) rather than a generic 'try again'. · Even where DPI fetch skips document upload, keep a lightweight liveness/face-match step everywhere (passive, <1s, no active gestures) as a separate always-on layer — DPI fetch proves the document is real, not that the person in front of the phone is its owner. · Reserve active liveness (turn head/blink/smile prompts) for a small high-risk tier (score-inconclusive passive result, prior fraud flags, high-value role) rather than making every candidate do it — this is where most time-to-verify and drop-off will concentrate if over-applied. · For MY and ID, design the face-match against a government-held reference photo (JPN/Dukcapil) as the primary trust signal, and treat the physical MyKad/KTP capture as a fallback path only, mirroring the direction both countries' infrastructure is already moving. · Instrument and expose vendor-style benchmarks (verification completion in seconds vs minutes, % choosing DPI-fetch vs manual path, cost/time saved) per country in the admin console — these numbers (MyInfo 'seconds', Wise 35% adoption, 70s/88-95% video-KYC conversion) are what ops/finance stakeholders will ask for to justify DPI investment per market. · Do not assume DigiLocker/Aadhaar removes the need for a selfie step in the India flow — plan UI/copy that clearly separates 'we fetched your verified ID' from 'now confirm it's you' so candidates don't expect the flow to end after document fetch.

  Source: https://www.idfy.com/the-ultimate-guide-to-background-verification-services/

  Source: https://ongrid.in/blogs/instant-bgv-how-ongrid-is-redefining-background-verification-for-fast-paced-hiring/
- **[number]** SpringVerify India's overall employment check runs 7-14 working days (basic checks 24-48 hrs, complex up to 2 weeks); criminal/court checks specifically take 2-7 days and are the long pole because verification requires contacting the nearest police station per the candidate's address and routing through a law-firm partner, with digitised metro courts resolving fast and non-digitised areas needing physical visits.   Source: https://in.springverify.com/blog/how-long-does-a-background-check-take/

  Source: https://asanify.com/global-employer-of-record/malaysia/background-check/

adjudication view (per-check verdicts, discrepancy notes) — mirrors HireRight's decoupled candidate vs. Applicant Center views and avoids exposing raw discrepancy data to candidates prematurely. · Design for asynchronous, per-check partial completion rather than one binary pass/fail: show a checklist (identity, address, employment, education, criminal) each progressing independently, since criminal/court checks alone can take 2-7 days while identity/address can clear in minutes — this also sets correct candidate expectations and reduces support pings. · Insert an explicit, timestamped consent + ID-capture step before dispatching any check (LOA in India, signed consent + MyKad/passport in Malaysia) — make this a hard gate in the flow, not a checkbox buried in T&Cs, both for compliance and for the audit trail ops will need. · Adopt a traffic-light (green/amber/red) per-check status with a defined amber threshold (~1-4% target) that routes automatically to a human review ## apac-compliance

- **[requirement]** India's DPDP Rules 2025 (notified 14 Nov 2025) require consent requests to be in clear, plain, itemized language stating exactly what personal data is collected and for which specific purpose, presented independently of any other unrelated information, with a facility to give, manage, review and withdraw consent as easily as it was given (via a Consent Manager registered with the Data Protection Board).  
  Source: https://www.roedl.com/en/insights/indias-dpdpa-2023-activates-with-2025-rules-revolutionizing-data-privacy-enforcement/
- **[requirement]** Under DPDPA, biometric data (fingerprints, facial scans) may only be collected for a 'compelling legitimate purpose' and consent must be specific, informed, and verifiable, with an easy opt-out at any time; verifiable consent is separately mandated when the data principal is a child or a person with disability.  
  Source: https://muhami.ae/articles/how-is-biometric-data-protected-under-indian-law/
- **[requirement]** UIDAI guidance holds that unmasked/full Aadhaar numbers cannot be stored by non-government entities; employers/verification vendors should collect and display only masked Aadhaar (first 8 of 12 digits hidden, last 4 visible), and Aadhaar Data Vault requirements (separate encrypted storage) apply specifically to 'Regulated Entities' referring Aadhaar data to UIDAI for verification, not to ordinary employers doing internal eKYC.  
  Source: https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=391c2c88-0ab3-4d29-b88d-1a53c68e45c9
- **[mechanic]** For Aadhaar-based eKYC, best practice is candidate-generated Virtual ID or masked Aadhaar submitted to UIDAI's system, with the verifying platform returning only a match/no-match result to HR/the employer — the employer's systems never see or store the unmasked 12-digit number.  
  Source: https://quinfy.com/aadhaar-kyc-verification-employee-background-screening/
- **[requirement]** Malaysia's PDPA Amendment Act 2024 (phased in 1 Jan 2025, 1 Apr 2025, 1 Jun 2025) formally classifies biometric data (facial recognition, fingerprint, voice, retinal, keystroke, gaze/eye-tracking, signature analysis) as 'sensitive personal data,' requiring explicit (opt-in, not implied) consent plus updated privacy notices and enhanced security safeguards specifically for that data category.  
  Source: https://tsl-legal.com/navigating-the-malaysian-personal-data-protection-amendment-act-2024-key-changes-implications-and-implications-for-businesses/
- **[requirement]** Singapore's PDPA generally prohibits organizations from collecting, using, holding copies of, or requiring disclosure of NRIC numbers except where legally required or 'necessary to accurately establish/verify identity to a high degree of fidelity'; a job application form must NOT ask for NRIC — it may only be collected once the candidate has actually accepted an offer and is commencing employment, and must be deleted once no longer needed for legal/business purposes (no fixed retention period is prescribed, so employers must set and justify their own).  
  Source: https://www.hireright.com/blog/singapore-pdpa-nric-id-check-background-screening
- **[number]** Indonesia's UU PDP classifies biometric and genetic data as 'special/sensitive' personal data requiring stricter protection and valid, withdrawable consent before processing; non-compliance (found only after the 17 Oct 2024 transition deadline) can trigger administrative sanctions — written warning, temporary processing suspension, forced deletion of unlawfully processed data, and fines up to 2% of annual revenue — plus separate criminal sanctions for intentional misuse or illegal disclosure.  
  Source: https://www.fortra.com/blog/navigating-indonesias-personal-data-protection-law-key-takeaways-organizations

**Design implications:** Build one reusable 'granular consent screen' component per country, not a single generic checkbox: India needs itemized purpose-by-purpose consent + visible withdraw/manage link (Consent Manager pattern); Malaysia needs a separate explicit opt-in toggle specifically for biometric/facial capture, distinct from the general T&Cs consent; Indonesia needs the same explicit sensitive-data opt-in plus a visible 'withdraw consent' action in-app. · For India, default the document-upload and display flows to masked Aadhaar (last 4 digits only) end-to-end — capture via Virtual ID/masked flow, store nothing unmasked, and show only a match/no-match badge in the ops console rather than the underlying number, since non-government entities should not persist full Aadhaar. · For Singapore/Malaysia hires, do not request NRIC or any national ID number at the initial job-application/interest stage of the chat flow — gate that field to unlock only after an offer is confirmed, and add a scheduled/automatic deletion job (retention timer) for NRIC and other national IDs tied to employment status, since no statutory retention period exists and the burden is on BetterPlace to justify how long it's kept. · Add a persistent 'why we need this' + retention-period microcopy under every biometric capture screen (selfie liveness check, video interview) across all four countries, since Malaysia/Indonesia/India all now treat biometric data as a distinct sensitive category requiring its own explicit consent rather than being bundled into a general privacy policy acceptance. · Build a compliance-metadata layer per candidate record (country, consent timestamp, purpose list version, retention/deletion due-date, masked-ID-only flag) so the admin/ops console can prove verifiable consent and enforce auto-purge — this is required to defend against DPDPA Data Protection Board inquiries and Indonesia's forced-deletion sanction risk. · Localize consent copy language-by-language (not just legal boilerplate translation) since India's DPDP Rules explicitly require 'clear and plain language' independent of unrelated info — a dense bilingual legal wall of text will fail this bar even if technically translated.
