AI Labs × Frontline Workforce Operations

Ten problem statements. Nine are ours to solve.

The funnel leaks at every stage where we make a frontline worker fight a form, wait in silence, or show up on hope. Every one of those moments is a conversation — and conversations are exactly what AI Labs ships. Six problems are solvable on the platform as it stands; three ride roadmap items already specced and prototyped.
